Kids hate brushing their teeth. So we made it a game.

We asked 70 primary-school children what would make brushing fun, then designed Brushy around their answers: a free educational app that turns a boring routine into a daily adventure.

01 · The problem

Kids know brushing matters. They still skip it.

Our survey of 70 primary-school children showed a clear gap between what kids know and what they actually do.

61%

have already had a cavity

41%

think you only need a dentist when a tooth hurts

39%

simply forget to brush

Why they skip it

Scaring kids with cavities doesn't work. The real reasons are everyday ones, and those can be designed for.

  • They forget 39%
  • Too tired in the evening 37%
  • It's boring 27%

02 · The insight

Don't lecture kids. Give them what they asked for.

Instead of telling kids what to do, we asked what motivates them. The answers shaped every part of Brushy, designed for ages 7–11 and grounded in science and child psychology.

72%

are motivated by a clean, beautiful smile

25%

explicitly asked for an app with a timer and rewards

  • 🎮

    Play, not lectures

    Learning happens through games and quizzes, not warnings.

  • ⏱️

    Guided, not guessed

    A visual timer walks kids through all four areas of the mouth in two minutes.

  • 💎

    Rewards that build habits

    Forming a habit takes about 66 days on average, and the reward economy is built to last that long.

  • 🔒

    Parents stay in control

    A PIN-protected parent panel, with all data stored on the device.

03 · The app

Five features that make brushing the best part of the day

04 · For parents

Designed for kids, built for parents.

A PIN-protected parent panel lets you track brushing on a monthly calendar, set brushing length to 2 or 3 minutes, and schedule morning and evening reminders around your family's routine.

  • Monthly brushing calendar
  • 2 or 3 minute sessions
  • Morning & evening reminders
🔒
Privacy by design

All data stays on your device. Nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

Grounded in science

More than just another app

Brushy is the result of applied, data-driven research developed in collaboration between the Faculty of Dental Medicine and Health Osijek and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Computer Science and Information Technology Osijek (FERIT). It respects children's cognitive psychology and targets a real problem: the gap between what kids know and how they behave.
