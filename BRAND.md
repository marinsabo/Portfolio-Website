# Brand

Plain, intentional, human. The content is the design: nothing is added unless it helps someone read, and everything that is there looks the same everywhere.

## Principles

1. **Content first.** If something only decorates, it goes.
2. **One way to do each thing.** One colour for text, one for links, one kind of line, one layout. A new page reuses what exists.
3. **Written by me.** Every word is mine. AI may fix typos or write code, never the voice.
4. **Honest.** Real numbers, real dates, and the plain truth, including "the business has since closed".

## Look

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#fef8ec` | Background |
| Ink | `#41424c` | All text, all lines |
| Link | `#284283` | Links only, underlined |

- **Type:** the default serif. Bold for the name and headings, regular for everything else. No webfonts.
- **Layout:** one centred column, at most 48rem wide. Name, quote and headings are centred; body text is left-aligned.
- **Lines:** 2px solid ink rules separate blocks: under the header, between navs, under every section. No other dividers.
- **Images:** rare, full column width, 5px corners. Never decorative.
- **Never:** shadows, gradients, icons, emoji, animations, cards, badges, accent colours.
- **Mark:** a bold serif "M" over a short ink rule, on paper (`favicon.svg`).

## Voice

- First person, short plain sentences. Say what happened and what I did.
- Concrete over impressive: "70 pupils", "185,000+ followers", "Jul 2026 – Sep 2026".
- No buzzwords, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- Calm confidence; dry humour is welcome, selling is not.
- Every page opens with one quote, in quotation marks, credited after an em dash: `“…” — Alan Kay`.

## Patterns

- **Entry:** title `Role @ Organisation` (projects: just the name, linked if live), then `Role:`, `Duration:` (projects: `Date:`), `Stack used:`, then one description paragraph.
- **Dates:** `Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY`, en dash with spaces; `now` for ongoing.
- **Lists inside a line:** `Design | Development | SEO` for roles, commas for stacks.
- **Names:** GitHub, LinkedIn, Credly, Db2, z/OS.
- **Page titles:** `Page · Marin Sabo`.
