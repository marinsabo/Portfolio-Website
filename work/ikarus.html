The agency

Ikarus Agency was the web development agency I ran with a friend, and where my client work started in 2023.

The problem

A new agency has to look credible before it has much to show. We needed a brand identity that set us apart and a website that showed what we could do.

What I did

  1. Created brand guidelines: logo, colour palette and typography.
  2. Designed the website in Figma to present our services and work.
  3. Built a responsive site with clear navigation and calls to action, and rolled the brand out across our digital channels.
