Ikarus Agency

A new brand identity and website for a digital marketing agency that wanted to compete for bigger clients.

Client
Ikarus Agency, digital marketing
My role
Branding, web design, development
Type
Rebrand & website
Deliverables
Brand guidelines, website

The client

Ikarus Agency helps businesses improve their online presence through branding, web design and digital marketing.

The problem

The agency didn’t have a brand identity that set it apart, and its old website didn’t show what it could do. That made it hard to win higher-value clients.

What I did

  1. Created brand guidelines: logo, colour palette and typography.
  2. Designed a new website in Figma that presents the agency’s services and work.
  3. Built a responsive site with clear navigation and calls to action, and rolled the new brand out across their digital channels.

Process

  1. Brand discovery
  2. Brand development
  3. Website design
  4. Development
  5. Launch
