WaveLance

A landing page that turns visitors from LinkedIn into booked discovery calls for a personal-branding agency.

Type

Live site

The client

WaveLance, led by founder Afreen, helps experts stand out on LinkedIn through ghostwriting, video editing, newsletters and profile optimisation.

The problem

The site had to be the hub for prospects coming from LinkedIn. That meant explaining a wide range of services clearly, proving they work, and making it easy to book a discovery call.

What I did

  1. Structured the page as a clear path: the problem (low visibility), then WaveLance’s services, then booking a discovery call.
  2. Designed a “Numbers Don’t Lie” section that shows client growth in charts.
  3. Placed testimonials and the founder’s story where they build trust before the call to action.
  4. Set up contact forms and booking links, and optimised the layout for mobile and desktop.

Process
