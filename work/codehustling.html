The client

CodeHustling is an Instagram community of 185,000+ followers, led by Fabion Kallaku, who publishes content about web development and the web industry.

The problem

Information about Fabion and the community was scattered across platforms. He needed one website that brought it all together, kept followers engaged, and had reliable hosting behind it.

What I did

  1. Planned the content structure so all community resources live in one place.
  2. Designed the site in Figma around the community, with clear calls to action.
  3. Built the site, made it easy to update, and set up SEO.
  4. Set up hosting and the domain, and maintained the site monthly after launch.
