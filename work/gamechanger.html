GameChanger

A fast, visually bold website for a gaming and Web3 conference, delivered on a deadline set by the event’s marketing plan.

The client

GameChanger organises conferences on the gaming industry and Web3, bringing together industry experts to discuss upcoming trends. The conference is funded by ticket sales, so the website directly affects whether the event succeeds.

The problem

Launch had to match the conference marketing plan, so the deadline was short. There were over 100 speakers and sponsors to present clearly, and the gaming and Web3 event space is visually crowded.

What I did

  1. Designed a vibrant, gaming-inspired look in Figma.
  2. Built it on WordPress with a custom theme, which allowed fast development without sacrificing performance.
  3. Set up SEO and hosting for launch.

Process

  2. Market research
  3. Design
  4. Development
  5. SEO & launch
