DartZ

Role: Db2 data model | Test data | Db2 & z/OS admin

Date: Jul 2026 – Sep 2026

Stack used: z/OS, COBOL, CICS, Db2, JCL, IBM DBB, Zowe

A darts tournament and league system built by our team during my internship at CROZ. CICS runs the transactions and screens, COBOL holds the business logic, Db2 stores the players, matches and leagues, and JCL handles builds and batch work. I designed the entire Db2 data model every program used, created the team’s test data and kept the environment running. Afterwards we moved the source into Git, and every push now builds the affected programs with IBM DBB and delivers them to z/OS through Zowe.

Brushy

Role: Research | UX design

Date: Apr 2026

Stack used: Figma

A case study website for Brushy, a mobile app concept that turns kids' toothbrushing into a game. The concept came out of a research project between the Faculty of Dental Medicine and Health Osijek and FERIT Osijek, where we surveyed 70 primary-school children about why they skip brushing and what would make it fun. The website presents that research, the insights behind the design, and the app's screens, in both English and Croatian. Built in collaboration with colleague Kiara Lemac from the Faculty of Dental Medicine and Health Osijek.
