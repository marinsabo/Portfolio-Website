Why this design?

I have been building websites for years. Ever since I first learned about web development back in high school, I have loved it because it allowed people to express themselves. Every website was different. Of course, there were certain trends and best practices, but in general, it was hard to find two identical websites. Because of that, it was very easy to get a sense of a person or company based on their website. It was an expression of themselves.

With the rapid development of AI, it has become surprisingly hard to express yourself. The main premise of AI was that we would be the authors and do the creative work, and AI would fix the typos and grammar mistakes. But ironically, it couldn’t have been further from the truth. Nowadays, AI is the author, and people are there merely to check it for any mistakes. And of all IT sectors, web development took the biggest hit. When was the last time, besides today, that you saw a website that stood out? Exactly. Individuality is dead, and we have decided to kill it.

I fell into the same trap. My previous website was also AI-enhanced. I wanted AI to “optimize” it for recruiters, for better job and internship opportunities. At the same time, I wanted it to “feel” more like me, not realizing that those two goals pull in opposite directions. With each prompt, it drifted further and further away from who I really am and became just one more generic site with the same features and design decisions as all the others.

That is why I decided to make a drastic change and build my website just the way that I like it: intentional, simple and human-friendly. I stopped asking myself what others would like to see and focused on what I would like to see. And this design is the answer.

I may allow AI to fix my mistakes or write a piece of code, but I will never let it take the most valuable thing that I own: my true self.
